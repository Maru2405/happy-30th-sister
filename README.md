# Sister's Sweet 30

"Create a one-page website celebrating my older sister's 30th birthday. She's the eldest of three siblings, and I'm the youngest — there's a 10-year age gap between us, so I've looked up to her my whole life. Include a warm, festive design (not a generic template look) with a bold '30' as a hero moment, a heartfelt letter section from a younger sibling's perspective — reflecting on watching her grow up, all the guidance/protection over the years given the age gap, and admiration for the woman she's become — a photo gallery with 3-4 image placeholders I can drop real pictures into, and a short 'wishes for her 30th year' list. Give it a cohesive color palette and typography, subtle celebratory animation (like confetti), and make sure it's mobile-responsive. Keep all copy easy for me to personalize with her name and real memories."
